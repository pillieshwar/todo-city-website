Every capture drops a card down from the TodoCity icon in the menu bar. Nothing is saved until you add it, so you always get the last word.

![The capture card: from Slack, the task Send Q3 numbers to Priya, read as Friday at 3:00 PM, with a project and section picked](/docs/img/capture-card-project.webp "The card, with the task's project and section.")

## What the card shows

- **Where it came from**, like **From Slack · New task**.
- **The task's title**, ready for you to edit.
- **+ Add description** for notes, links or details.
- **How TodoCity read the date**, like “Read “by Friday 3pm” as Friday · 3:00 PM.”
- **The day**: the day TodoCity read, plus **Today**, **Tomorrow**, another nearby day, **No date** and **More…** for a calendar.
- **The time**, and a **repeat** when the text had one.
- **Project** and **Section**, to file it right away.
- **Selected text**: open it to see the exact words you captured.

While the model reads the text, the card says **Reading it on this Mac…**.

## Add it

:::steps
1. **Change anything** you like: the title, the day, the time, the project.
2. **Press <kbd>Return</kbd>**, or click **Add task**.
:::

To let it go, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click **Discard**.

## When a date could mean two days

Some phrases fit more than one day. “Next Friday” on a Tuesday could be this week's Friday or next week's, and `3/4` could be March 4 or April 3. TodoCity asks instead of guessing: the card offers both readings, and you pick one.

When the text names no clear date, the card asks **When is this due?** so you can choose. See [Dates and times](/docs/dates-and-times).

## Fill in the card yourself

Before you add an AI model, or while it's downloading, the card opens with the text you captured so you can write the task yourself. Add the model in **Settings → Open models**. See [AI models](/docs/ai-models).

## Back to the source

Each task remembers where it came from: the Slack thread, the email, the web page. A small chip on the task, like **from Slack** or **from Mail**, opens it in one click. With a task selected, press <kbd>O</kbd>.

![A task in Today with its from Slack chip](/docs/img/source-chip.webp "The source chip opens the message the task came from.")

- **Show where tasks came from** in **Settings → General** shows the chip **Always**, **On hover** or **Never**.
- **Remember where tasks came from** in **Settings → Capture & shortcuts** keeps the app and link with each new capture.

> [!NOTE]
> Captured text is treated as plain information, never as instructions. The model can only suggest a task, which you review on this card.
