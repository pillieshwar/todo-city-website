**Calendar** shows your week, every hour of every day, with your tasks beside your calendar events. Each task sits at its real start and length, so you can see where your time goes.

![Calendar: a week from Sunday to Saturday, with untimed tasks in Any time and timed tasks and meetings at their hours](/docs/img/calendar-week.webp "Calendar, a week at a time.")

## Find your way around

- **Any time**, at the top of each day, holds the day's tasks with no time.
- **This week** and **Next week** at the top move a week at a time, with arrows for the week before or after.
- To move a day at a time, drag the row of dates sideways or swipe with two fingers. The <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> keys do the same.
- Short tasks stay on one tidy line; point at one to see its full title. A crowded hour folds the rest into **+2**.

## Plan tasks

- **Drag a task** to another day or time. Drops snap to 5 minutes.
- **Drag a task's bottom edge** to make it longer or shorter.
- **Click anywhere in an hour** to add a task there.
- **Tasks with no date** wait beside the week: drag one onto a day or an hour to plan it.

## How long a task takes

Every task can have a length, from 1 minute to a whole day, and can start at any minute, like 9:07.

- Use the **Length** chip on a task: pick a quick length, or type minutes like `7`, `45` or `1h30`.
- Or type it when you add the task: “Stand-up tomorrow 9:07 for 7 min”.

## Meetings beside your tasks

Turn on **Show calendar events in TodoCity** in **Settings → Connectors → Apple Calendar**, and your meetings appear next to your tasks. They come from the calendars in the Calendar app on your Mac, including Google, Outlook and iCloud calendars you've added there. See [Apple Calendar](/docs/connectors/apple-calendar).

## Move your own meetings

Drag one of your own meetings to a new time or day, or stretch it, and it moves in Apple Calendar too. A note confirms the move, with **Undo** to put it back.

![Calendar after moving a meeting, with a note saying it moved to Monday at 1:00 PM and an Undo button](/docs/img/calendar-meeting-moved.webp "Moved, with Undo.")

- **Meetings you organize** ask first: **Move for everyone?**, since your guests get the new time.
- **Invitations from other people**, all-day events, read-only calendars and TodoCity's own calendars stay as they are in Calendar.
- For a repeating meeting, only the occurrence you dragged moves.

## The month beside your lists

In full screen, a month calendar sits on the right of your lists. Drag a task onto a date to plan it. Inside a project or a section, the month and the day's hours show only that project's (or section's) tasks.

Show or hide this pane with the button at the top right, or press <kbd>⌥⌘\\</kbd>.
