Search narrows the list you're looking at as you type. Press <kbd>/</kbd>, <kbd>⌘K</kbd> or <kbd>⌘F</kbd>, or click **Search** at the top of the sidebar.

![Search open above Today, with the #work and #home tags under the search box](/docs/img/search.webp "Search, with your tags one click away.")

## What it looks at

A task matches when every word you type appears in its:

- title,
- notes,
- original captured text,
- or the app it came from, like Slack or Mail.

Type `slack priya` to find the task captured from Slack that mentions Priya.

## Search by tag

Start a word with `#` to match a tag exactly: `#work` shows only tasks tagged #work. Your most-used tags sit under the search box: click one to add it, click again to take it off.

Your tags are listed in the sidebar too. Click one to search for it.

## Modes and finished tasks

While you search, the list includes every project, whatever [mode](/docs/modes) you're in. To search everything with no project, open **Inbox**; to search finished tasks, open **Completed**.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close search, or click **×** to clear it.
