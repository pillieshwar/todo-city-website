The first time you open TodoCity, a short tour walks you through three steps: **Model**, **Shortcut** and **Try it**. It takes about a minute, and you can change any of it later in Settings.

![The first step of the tour: Pick your on-device model, with the model that's best for this Mac selected](/docs/img/onboarding-model.webp "Step 1: TodoCity suggests the best model for your Mac.")

## 1. Model

**Pick your on-device model.** TodoCity uses a small AI model, running right on this Mac, to read the text you capture and write the task. The one marked **Best for this Mac** is the one that fits your Mac's memory.

- Click **Download and continue**. The model only downloads once, and you can keep going with the tour while it does.
- If you'd rather type your tasks yourself for now, click **Later — I'll type tasks myself**. You can add the model whenever you like in **Settings → Open models**.

## 2. Shortcut

**How do you want to capture?** Pick one:

- **Press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice** is the easiest. Copy as you normally would, press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> once more, and TodoCity picks it up. It works in every app and doesn't need any permission.
- **A shortcut, like <kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd>,** lets you skip copying altogether: just select text and press it. For this, TodoCity needs Accessibility access, so click **Allow in System Settings** and turn on TodoCity. [Install](/docs/install#permissions-todocity-asks-for) explains why.

## 3. Try it

The tour gives you a sample message to practise on:

> Could you send me the Q3 numbers by Friday 3pm? Thanks!

:::steps
1. **Select the message**, then press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice (or your shortcut).
2. **Check the card** that drops down from the menu bar. TodoCity has written the task and understood “by Friday 3pm” as Friday at 3:00 PM.
3. **Press Return.** That's your first task in TodoCity.
:::

Rather not select anything? Click **Or just use the sample** instead. When you're done, click **Start using TodoCity**.

If the model is still downloading, or you chose **Later**, the card opens for you to fill in yourself this time. Everything else works exactly the same.

> [!NOTE]
> Once you finish the tour, macOS asks whether TodoCity can send you notifications. Say yes if you'd like reminders when tasks are due. More in [Notifications](/docs/notifications).
