The first time you open TodoCity, a short tour sets it up in three steps: **Model**, **Shortcut** and **Try it**. It takes about a minute, and you can change every choice later in Settings.

![The first step of the tour: Pick your on-device model, with the model that's best for this Mac selected](/docs/img/onboarding-model.webp "Step 1: TodoCity suggests the best model for your Mac.")

## 1. Model

**Pick your on-device model.** TodoCity reads the text you capture and writes the task with a small AI model that runs on this Mac. The one marked **Best for this Mac** fits your Mac's memory.

- Click **Download and continue**. The model downloads once, and you can carry on with the tour while it does.
- Rather type your tasks yourself for now? Click **Later — I'll type tasks myself**. Add the model any time in **Settings → Open models**.

## 2. Shortcut

**How do you want to capture?** Pick one:

- **Press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice** (the easiest). Copy as usual, then press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> again, and TodoCity picks it up. It works in every app, with no permission needed.
- **A shortcut, like <kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd>.** Select text and press it, with no copying needed. The shortcut needs Accessibility access: click **Allow in System Settings**, then turn on TodoCity. See [Install](/docs/install#permissions-todocity-asks-for).

## 3. Try it

The tour shows a sample message:

> Could you send me the Q3 numbers by Friday 3pm? Thanks!

:::steps
1. **Select the message**, then press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice (or your shortcut).
2. **Check the card** that drops down from the menu bar. TodoCity has written the task and read “by Friday 3pm” as Friday at 3:00 PM.
3. **Press Return.** Your first task is in TodoCity.
:::

You can also click **Or just use the sample**. Then click **Start using TodoCity**.

If the model is still downloading, or you chose **Later**, the card opens for you to fill in yourself this time. Everything else works the same.

> [!NOTE]
> When you finish the tour, macOS asks whether TodoCity may send notifications, so it can remind you when tasks are due. See [Notifications](/docs/notifications).
