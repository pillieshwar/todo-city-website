Click a task and it opens in place, with everything it can hold. Change anything and it saves as you go: there's no Save button.

![A task opened in place: its title, notes, a link, a sub-task field, and chips for its project, day and time](/docs/img/task-open.webp "A task, opened in place.")

## What's in a task

| Part | What it's for | How to set it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | What to do | Click the title, or press <kbd>E</kbd> |
| Notes | Links, details, anything useful later. Links open in one click | **Notes**, or press <kbd>N</kbd> |
| Sub-tasks | Steps to tick off one by one | **Add a sub-task**, or press <kbd>C</kbd> |
| Project and section | Where it belongs | The project chip, or press <kbd>P</kbd> |
| Day and time | When you plan to do it | The date chip and **Time**, or press <kbd>D</kbd> |
| Length | How long it takes | **Length** |
| Deadline | The day it must be done by | The deadline icon |
| Priority | P1, P2 or P3 | The flag icon |
| Repeat | When it comes back | The repeat icon. See [Repeats](/docs/repeats) |
| Tags | Words to group and find tasks | The tag icon, or `#tags` in the title |

## Day and time

The date chip offers **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Someday** and **No date**, plus a calendar. Press <kbd>D</kbd> for the full date and time editor, which adds **This evening** and **Next week**, a time, a deadline and a repeat.

You can also type the date: “fri 3pm”, “tomorrow evening”, “every monday”. See [Dates and times](/docs/dates-and-times).

Times can be any minute, like 9:07.

## Sub-tasks

Sub-tasks are a checklist inside the task. In full screen, they show right under the task, so you can tick them without opening anything.

Paste or type a few lines as a list (`- milk`, `- eggs`) and each line becomes a sub-task. Capturing a bulleted list does the same.

To show or hide notes and sub-tasks under every task, use **Settings → Appearance → Show notes under tasks**.

## Deadline

A **deadline** is the day a task must be done by, separate from the day you plan to do it. A task planned for next week but due Friday shows in Today on Friday.

The task shows a chip like **Due Fri**, or **Due today** when the day comes. To clear it, open the deadline and click **Remove deadline**. A deadline on a repeating task ends the series on that day.

## Length

Use the **Length** chip to pick a quick length or type any number of minutes: `7`, `45`, `1h30`. A task can last from 1 minute to a whole day. [Calendar](/docs/calendar) draws each task at its real start and length.

## Priority

| Priority | Name |
| --- | --- |
| P1 | Urgent |
| P2 | High |
| P3 | Medium |

Click the flag, or type `p1`, `p2` or `p3` in a new task.

## Tags

Type a tag and press <kbd>Return</kbd>, or write `#tags` in the title. Your tags are listed in the sidebar: click one to show only its tasks. You can also search for `#home`. See [Search](/docs/search).

## More

The **⋯** button on an open task has the rest:

- **Open in Slack**, **Open in Mail** and so on, for captured tasks: back to where it came from.
- **Send to agent…**: hand the task to an AI agent. See [The agent panel](/docs/agents/panel).
- **Snooze**: 15 min, 1 hour, Tonight, Tomorrow or Next week.
- **Move to Someday**
- **Duplicate**
- **Delete**

<kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes the last change, including a delete.

## Saving

Edits save half a second after you stop typing, and when you click away or close the task. **Done** closes it.

## Select several

<kbd>⌘</kbd>-click or <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click to select several tasks (<kbd>⌘A</kbd> selects all), then complete, move or delete them together.
