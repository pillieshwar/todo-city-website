A repeating task comes back on its own. When you finish one, the next one appears on its next day.

## Pick a repeat

Open a task and click the repeat button, right after priority. Pick one of the presets:

- **Every day**
- **Every weekday** (Monday to Friday)
- **Every week**
- **Every 2 weeks**
- **Every month**
- **Every year**

Repeating tasks show a small repeat icon on their line.

You can also type the repeat when you add a task: “water the plants every monday”. See [Dates and times](/docs/dates-and-times#repeats).

![Adding the task “Book club the 2nd tuesday of every month”, read as Monthly, 2nd Tue](/docs/img/repeat-typed.webp "A repeat, typed in plain words.")

## Custom repeats

Click **Custom…** for anything else.

![The custom repeat editor: every 2 weeks, on Monday and Wednesday, ending after 6 times, with the next dates listed](/docs/img/repeat-custom.webp "A custom repeat, with the next days shown before you save.")

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Every** | A number of days, weeks, months or years: every 3 days, every 2 weeks |
| **On** | For weeks: the weekdays, like Monday and Wednesday |
| **The** | For months: a weekday, like the 2nd Tuesday, or **The last day** |
| **Next one** | **On schedule**, or **After I finish** to count from the day you finish it |
| **Ends** | **Never**, on a date, or after a number of times |

Below the choices, TodoCity lists the next days it will land on, like “5 more times, last one Wed, Dec 2.” Click **Done** to save.

### After I finish

With **After I finish**, the next one counts from the day you finish the task, not from its planned day. “Every 2 weeks, after I finish” suits things like watering plants or a haircut.

## Deadlines end a series

A repeating task with a deadline stops at its deadline. The repeat editor shows it: “Ends by the deadline, Dec 18.”

## Skip, pause or stop

The repeat button's menu has:

- **Skip next**: jump over the next one, without finishing it.
- **Pause**: the series waits in Someday, with no reminders, until you click **Resume**.
- **Stop**: the task stays, and stops repeating.
- **Edit**: change the repeat.

With a series that ends after a number of times, skipping one counts as one of them.

## Move one day, or the whole series

When you move a repeating task to another day, TodoCity asks which to move: **This one** or **All of them**.

## Repeats from Todoist and Things 3

Daily, weekday, weekly, every-2-weeks, monthly and yearly repeats come in as the same repeat. Custom repeats come once, with a note in the task so you can pick a repeat in TodoCity. See [Todoist](/docs/connectors/todoist) and [Things 3](/docs/connectors/things).
