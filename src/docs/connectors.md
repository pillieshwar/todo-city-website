**Settings → Connectors** links TodoCity with the apps you already use. Each card shows whether it's **Connected**; click **Connect** to set one up.

![Settings → Connectors with four cards: Todoist, Things 3, Apple Calendar (Connected) and Apple Reminders](/docs/img/settings-connectors.webp "Settings → Connectors.")

## The connectors

:::cards
- [Todoist](/docs/connectors/todoist) Bring your Todoist projects in and keep them in sync, both ways.
- [Things 3](/docs/connectors/things) Bring your Things library in and keep new to-dos flowing in.
- [Apple Reminders](/docs/connectors/apple-reminders) Sync both ways, and tick tasks off on your iPhone.
- [Apple Calendar](/docs/connectors/apple-calendar) Your meetings beside your tasks, and your tasks in Calendar.
:::

On each card, the **(i)** next to **How to move in** and **What goes where** shows the steps and how each part of the other app comes into TodoCity.

## More connections

These have their own pages in Settings:

| Where | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings → Your other Macs** | The same tasks on all your Macs, end-to-end encrypted. See [Sync between your Macs](/docs/sync-between-macs) |
| **Settings → Agents** | Connect Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other AI apps. See [AI agents](/docs/agents) |
| **Settings → Watch** | Suggests tasks from what's on your screen. See [Watch](/docs/watch) |

> [!PRIVATE]
> Every connector is off until you turn it on, and each one says where your data goes. See [Privacy](/docs/privacy).

## Before an import

When you bring in tasks from Todoist or Things 3, TodoCity backs itself up first. The backup is in **Settings → Backups & export**, marked **before import**, so you can undo it. See [Your data](/docs/data).
