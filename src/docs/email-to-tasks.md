There are three ways to turn your emails into tasks, from one email at a time to your whole inbox.

## Capture an email

Select the part of an email that asks something of you, and press <kbd>⌘C</kbd> twice. TodoCity writes the task and its date, and the task keeps a **from Mail** chip that opens the email again in one click. See [Capture](/docs/capture).

## Let Watch spot them

With [Watch](/docs/watch) on, TodoCity reads the email in front of you and suggests the requests, deadlines and promises it finds as tasks for you to approve. Watch now covers what the earlier **Email to tasks** feature did, for every app on your screen.

## Ask an agent, like Claude Code

If an agent you use can read your email, connect it to TodoCity and ask it to add the tasks:

:::steps
1. **Connect the agent** in **Settings → Agents**. See [AI agents](/docs/agents).
2. **Ask it**, in your own words: “go through today's emails and add a TodoCity task for each thing I need to do, with its due date”.
3. **Approve the tasks.** Each one waits in the menu bar until you add it.
:::

You can also start Claude Code from TodoCity's [agent panel](/docs/agents/panel), where it's already connected.

> [!PRIVATE]
> Capture and Watch with the open model stay on your Mac. An agent reads your email with its own access and sends what it reads to its AI service; TodoCity only receives the tasks it adds.
