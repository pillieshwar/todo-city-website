**Today** puts everything for today in one list. Anything late sits at the top, then come today's tasks, and then **This Evening**.

![Today in full screen: Overdue, then Today's tasks with their times, then This Evening, with this week's dates and the day's hours on the right](/docs/img/today.webp "Today, with this week and the day's hours beside it.")

## What shows in Today

- **Overdue** holds tasks whose day, time or deadline has already passed. Click **Move to today** to bring them all forward at once, or replan them one by one.
- **Today** lists the tasks planned for today, in time order. A task planned for later still shows up here if its **deadline** is today.
- **This Evening** is for tasks you've saved for later in the day. To put one there, select it, press <kbd>D</kbd> and pick **This evening**.

When you finish a task, it moves out of the way. A line like **1 completed today · Show** brings finished tasks back into view. If you'd rather keep them in the list, turn on **Settings → Appearance → Show completed tasks**.

## Plan with the hours

In full screen, this week's dates and the day's hours sit right beside Today, so you can plan by dragging.

- **Drag a task onto an hour** to give it that time.
- **Drag a task onto a date** to move it to that day.
- A dot under a date means something's planned for that day. A red dot means something's late.
- Click an hour to add a task at that time.

If you turn on Apple Calendar, your meetings show up in the hours as well. See [Apple Calendar](/docs/connectors/apple-calendar).

## In the side panel

In the side panel, Today has a week strip across the top, and you can drag a task onto another day to move it there. The buttons at the top switch Today between a **List** and **Hours**, or you can press <kbd>V</kbd>.

![The side panel showing Today in hours, with untimed tasks at the top and timed tasks at their hours](/docs/img/today-hours-panel.webp "Today in hours, in the side panel.")

## Move tasks quickly

Select a task and use these keys:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>T</kbd> | Move to today |
| <kbd>M</kbd> | Move to tomorrow |
| <kbd>W</kbd> | Move to next week |
| <kbd>S</kbd> | Snooze |
| <kbd>X</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> | Complete |

Don't like these keys? You can choose your own in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Modes

When you're in a [mode](/docs/modes) like **Work**, Today only shows that mode's projects, plus tasks that aren't in any project. A line tells you how many more are waiting outside the mode, and **Show** brings them in.
