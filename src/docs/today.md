**Today** shows everything for today in one list: anything late at the top, then today's tasks, then **This Evening**.

![Today in full screen: Overdue, then Today's tasks with their times, then This Evening, with this week's dates and the day's hours on the right](/docs/img/today.webp "Today, with this week and the day's hours beside it.")

## What shows in Today

- **Overdue**: tasks whose day, time or deadline has passed. Click **Move to today** to bring them all to today, or plan each one again.
- **Today**: tasks planned for today, in time order. A task planned for later whose **deadline** is today shows here too.
- **This Evening**: tasks you've set for this evening. Select a task, press <kbd>D</kbd> and pick **This evening**.

Finished tasks step aside: a line like **1 completed today · Show** brings them back into view. To keep finished tasks in the list, turn on **Settings → Appearance → Show completed tasks**.

## Plan with the hours

In full screen, this week's dates and the day's hours sit beside Today.

- **Drag a task onto an hour** to give it that time.
- **Drag a task onto a date** to move it to that day.
- A dot under a date means something's planned that day; a red one means something's late.
- Click an hour to add a task at that time.

Your meetings from Apple Calendar show in the hours too, when you turn them on. See [Apple Calendar](/docs/connectors/apple-calendar).

## In the side panel

The side panel shows Today with a week strip at the top: drag a task onto another day to move it. Switch Today between a **List** and **Hours** with the buttons at the top, or press <kbd>V</kbd>.

![The side panel showing Today in hours, with untimed tasks at the top and timed tasks at their hours](/docs/img/today-hours-panel.webp "Today in hours, in the side panel.")

## Move tasks quickly

With a task selected:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>T</kbd> | Move to today |
| <kbd>M</kbd> | Move to tomorrow |
| <kbd>W</kbd> | Move to next week |
| <kbd>S</kbd> | Snooze |
| <kbd>X</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> | Complete |

You can pick your own keys. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Modes

In a [mode](/docs/modes) like **Work**, Today shows only that mode's projects and tasks with no project. A line tells you how many more are waiting outside the mode, with **Show** to see them.
