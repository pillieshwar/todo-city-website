**Settings → Connectors → Apple Reminders** keeps your tasks and a Reminders list in step, both ways. TodoCity is a Mac app, so this is how your tasks reach your iPhone: tick a task off in Reminders there, and it's done in TodoCity too.

![The Apple Reminders card: Sync with Apple Reminders on, the TodoCity list, synced through iCloud, with Sync now](/docs/img/reminders-sync.webp "Apple Reminders, syncing both ways.")

## Turn it on

:::steps
1. **Open Settings → Connectors → Apple Reminders** and click **Connect**. When macOS asks, allow TodoCity to use Reminders.
2. **Pick a list** for your tasks: **New list “TodoCity”**, or one you already have.
3. **Choose the options** below.
4. **Click Turn on sync.**
:::

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Add my open tasks too** | Copies the tasks already in TodoCity to the list |
| **Include the original text** | Puts the text you captured in each reminder's notes |

## How it syncs

- **Both ways.** Add, change or tick off a task in either app, and the other follows.
- **Deleting a task in TodoCity deletes its reminder.** Deleting a reminder keeps the task in TodoCity.
- **Sync now** in the card syncs straight away. The card shows when it last synced.
- Tasks you bring in from Todoist or Things 3 aren't sent to Reminders on their own.

## On your iPhone

Pick a list in your iCloud account, and Reminders on your iPhone, iPad and other Macs shows your tasks. TodoCity asks you to confirm first: **OK to copy tasks to iCloud**.

To keep everything on this Mac instead, pick an **On My Mac** list.

> [!PRIVATE]
> With an iCloud list, task titles and dates sync through iCloud, so they leave this Mac. With an **On My Mac** list, they're stored only on this Mac. The card always says which.

## With two Macs

With [sync between your Macs](/docs/sync-between-macs), each Mac keeps its own links to Reminders. A task deleted on one Mac deletes its reminder too, so it stays deleted everywhere.
