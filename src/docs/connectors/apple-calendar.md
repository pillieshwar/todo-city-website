**Settings → Connectors → Apple Calendar** does two things, each on its own switch: it shows your meetings beside your tasks, and it can put your tasks in Calendar.

## Show your meetings in TodoCity

:::steps
1. **Open Settings → Connectors → Apple Calendar** and click **Connect**. When macOS asks, allow TodoCity to use your calendars.
2. **Turn on Show calendar events in TodoCity.**
3. **Tick the calendars to show** under **Calendars to show**.
:::

Your meetings appear next to your tasks in Today and [Calendar](/docs/calendar). They come from the Calendar app on your Mac, so Google, Outlook and iCloud calendars you've added there are included. You can drag your own meetings to move them; everything else stays as it is in Calendar.

Each [mode](/docs/modes) can show its own calendars too.

> [!PRIVATE]
> Your events stay on this Mac. TodoCity reads them from the Calendar app to show them.

## Put your tasks in Calendar

Turn on **Put my tasks in Calendar**, and tasks with a day and a time show as events in Apple Calendar.

![The Your tasks in Calendar section: syncing in iCloud to 4 calendars, with TodoCity · Work and TodoCity · Personal groups and their projects ticked](/docs/img/calendar-sync.webp "Your tasks in Calendar, one calendar per project.")

- **One calendar per project.** TodoCity's calendars are grouped by mode, like **TodoCity · Work · Launch** and **TodoCity · Personal · Home**. Tick them in Calendar to show or hide each one. Your default calendar is never used.
- **Choose which projects** go to Calendar with the boxes under each group, or **Sync every** project in a group. New projects are added by themselves.
- **Keep them in** an account: **iCloud** shows them on your iPhone too; **On My Mac** keeps them here.

### Both ways

- **Move or stretch an event** in Calendar, and the task's time and length follow.
- **Delete the event**, and the task goes too.
- Change the task in TodoCity, and its event follows.

A safety brake holds back a large batch of deletes, so a slip in Calendar can't empty your lists.

### What stays out of Calendar

- Tasks with no time.
- Projects under **Keep on this Mac only** in **Settings → Your other Macs**.

TodoCity's own calendars stay out of the meetings it shows you, so a task never appears twice.

> [!PRIVATE]
> With an iCloud account, your tasks' events sync through iCloud, so they leave this Mac. Pick **On My Mac** to keep them local. **Keep on this Mac only** projects never go to Calendar.

## With two Macs

Each event carries a link to its task, so two Macs that both put tasks in Calendar link to the same event instead of adding it twice.
