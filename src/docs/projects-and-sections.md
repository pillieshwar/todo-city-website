A **project** holds the tasks for one goal, like “Offsite 2026” or “Q4 launch”. A **section** splits a project into parts, like “Travel” and “Program”.

![A project shown as a board with three columns: General, Program and Travel, and one task open on the right](/docs/img/project.webp "A project as a board, with its sections as columns.")

## Start a project

- Click **+** next to **Projects** in the sidebar and name it. A project can start before it has any tasks.
- Or type `@Name` in a new task to put it in a project.

**Add Task** at the top right picks the **project**, **section**, **date** and **time** right in the form.

## Sections

Open a project in the sidebar to see its sections, listed like chat channels: **# General**, **# Program**, **# Travel**, each with how many tasks are still to do. **General** holds the project's tasks that aren't in a section.

- **Add a section** from the project's **⋯** menu (**Add a section…**), or with **+ New section** when you pick where a task goes.
- **Click a section** for its own page. Finished tasks stay there, crossed out, and **Add Task** puts the new task straight in that section.
- **Drag a task** from one section to another in the project's list, or onto a project or section in the sidebar.

![A section's own page: Pack for the offsite with its sub-tasks, and Book flights, with the calendar showing only this section's tasks](/docs/img/section.webp "A section's page, with the calendar for just this section.")

## Give a project its own color

Pick **Color** in the project's **⋯** menu. Its tasks' checkboxes, calendar blocks and dots on the month take that color. **Default color** sets it back.

## The calendar follows you

Inside a project, the month and the day's hours on the right show only that project's tasks. Inside a section, only that section's.

## List or board

The **View** button at the top shows a project as a **List** or a **Board**, with a column for each section.

## Rename or delete

Use the **⋯** menu next to a project (**Rename project…**) or a section (**Rename section…**).

To delete a project or a section, move or delete its tasks first. Then **Delete project** or **Delete section** is in the same menu.

## Projects that sync

Projects that came from Todoist or Things 3 and keep in sync have **Stop syncing** in their **⋯** menu. Their tasks stay in TodoCity. See [Connectors](/docs/connectors).

Every project also shows up in your city. See [Your city](/docs/city).
