The lists in the sidebar each show your tasks in a different way. You'll see **Today**, **Calendar**, **Inbox**, **Overdue** and **Completed**, followed by your projects. Today and Calendar have pages of their own: [Today](/docs/today) and [Calendar](/docs/calendar).

## Inbox

**Inbox** holds every task that isn't in a project, grouped by when it's due:

| Group | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Tasks for today, plus anything late |
| Tomorrow | Tasks for tomorrow |
| Next 7 days | The rest of the week ahead |
| Later | Everything after that |
| Someday | Tasks with no date |

![Inbox: tasks with no project, grouped into Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days and Someday](/docs/img/inbox.webp "Inbox, as a list.")

You can show Inbox as a **List** or a **Board** using the **View** button at the top. On the board, drag a task into another column to change its day.

## Someday

**Someday** is the place for ideas you haven't planned yet, and it's the last group in Inbox.

- If you add a task with no date and no project, it goes straight to Someday.
- You can also pick **Someday** from a task's date menu, or **Move to Someday** from its **⋯** menu. Someday tasks stay out of Today and Calendar until you plan them.
- Paused repeating tasks wait here too.

## Overdue

**Overdue** gathers every open task whose day, time or deadline has passed, and the sidebar shows how many there are. That gives you one place to check, rather than a banner on every screen.

![The Overdue list with one task, and Today's tasks below it](/docs/img/overdue.webp "Overdue, in one place.")

- **Move to today** brings them all to today in one click.
- Or drag one onto the calendar to plan it again, or just tick it off.

Someday tasks and paused tasks never count as overdue.

## Completed

**Completed** keeps every task you've finished, with the newest at the top. Click **Show earlier** to go further back. If you want a task back, un-tick it and it reopens right where it was.

Finished tasks also step out of the other lists. A line like **2 completed · Show** brings them back into view where they were. If you'd rather they stayed put, turn on **Settings → Appearance → Show completed tasks**.

## With agents

**With agents** shows up once you've handed tasks to an AI agent. See [The agent panel](/docs/agents/panel).
