The sidebar's lists show your tasks in different ways: **Today**, **Calendar**, **Inbox**, **Overdue** and **Completed**, then your projects. Today and Calendar have their own pages: [Today](/docs/today) and [Calendar](/docs/calendar).

## Inbox

**Inbox** holds every task that isn't in a project, grouped by when:

| Group | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Tasks for today, and anything late |
| Tomorrow | Tasks for tomorrow |
| Next 7 days | The rest of the week ahead |
| Later | Everything after that |
| Someday | Tasks with no date |

![Inbox: tasks with no project, grouped into Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days and Someday](/docs/img/inbox.webp "Inbox, as a list.")

Show Inbox as a **List** or a **Board** from the **View** button at the top. On the board, drag a task to another column to change its day.

## Someday

**Someday** is for ideas you haven't planned yet. It's the last group in Inbox.

- A task you add with no date and no project goes straight to Someday.
- Pick **Someday** in a task's date menu, or **Move to Someday** in its **⋯** menu. Someday tasks stay out of Today and Calendar until you plan them.
- Paused repeating tasks wait here too.

## Overdue

**Overdue** gathers every open task whose day, time or deadline has passed, with a count in the sidebar. That's the one place to look, instead of a banner on every screen.

![The Overdue list with one task, and Today's tasks below it](/docs/img/overdue.webp "Overdue, in one place.")

- **Move to today** brings them all to today in one click.
- Drag one onto the calendar to plan it again, or tick it off.

Someday and paused tasks never count as overdue.

## Completed

**Completed** keeps every task you've finished, newest first. Click **Show earlier** to go further back. To bring one back, un-tick it: it's open again, in the same place.

Finished tasks also leave the other lists. A line like **2 completed · Show** shows them where they were. To keep them in place, turn on **Settings → Appearance → Show completed tasks**.

## With agents

**With agents** appears when you've handed tasks to an AI agent. See [The agent panel](/docs/agents/panel).
